# Database of Religious History

The Database of Religious History (DRH) describes itself as "the world's first comprehensive online quantitative and qualitative encyclopedia of religious cultural history". Its browse page lists recently published entries about religious groups, with the period covered and, where given, the region. Entries are written by scholars, by Ph.D. research assistants from literature reviews (secondary-source entries), or by members of a movement through the New Religiosity Poll (citizen-science entries).
